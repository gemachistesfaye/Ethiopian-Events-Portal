![Culture Zone](../assets/screenshot4.png)

## Overview
Immerse yourself in Ethiopian heritage through our gamified Culture Zone.

## Key Features
- **AI-Generated Trivia:** Test your knowledge with dynamic multiple-choice questions spanning history, geography, and traditions.
- **Thematic Exploration:** Browse interactive cards focusing on authentic Ethiopian food, music, and ancient historical events.
- **Offline Reliability:** A local pool of 100 questions ensures you can always access trivia even without a network connection.
