# AI Heritage Guide Chatbot

![AI Chatbot](../assets/screenshot3.png)

## Overview
Powered by the Google Gemini API, our context-aware conversational guide brings Ethiopian history to life.

## Key Features
- **Dynamic Roles:** Choose between different personas (storyteller, teacher, guide).
- **Deep Insights:** Ask complex questions about Ethiopian history, traditions, and geography.
- **Voice Support:** High-fidelity Amharic text-to-speech for correct pronunciation.
